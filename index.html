Kaun-kaun se fraud par nazar rakhi ja rahi hai?

Digital Arrest & Impersonation

Investment & Trading Fraud

Fake trading apps, task-based investment, binary/colour prediction claims aur Ponzi-style offers.

Phishing & Fake Links

UPI & Payment Fraud

Job & Task Scams

Social Media & Identity Fraud

Sextortion & Cyber Blackmail

Fraudulent loan apps, hidden charges, data misuse and threatening recovery tactics.

Lottery, Prize & Courier Fraud

Fake winnings, parcel/customs calls and advance-fee demands.

Common Questions

SochSathi aur online safety se jude aam sawaalon ke seedhe jawaab.

Kya SochSathi kisi platform ko bina proof scam declare karta hai?

Nahi. Hum available evidence, public sources aur risk indicators ko alag rakhte hain. Har allegation ko verified fact nahi maana jata.

Kya TRUST CHECK AI ka result 100% sahi hota hai?

Nahi. AI galti kar sakta hai. Iska analysis ek initial safety aid hai; important decisions se pehle official aur independent sources se verify karein.

Agar online paise ka fraud ho gaya to pehle kya karein?

Turant 1930 par call karein, cybercrime.gov.in par report karein aur apne bank/payment provider ko inform karein. Transaction IDs aur screenshots sambhal kar rakhein.

Kya OTP, UPI PIN ya password TRUST CHECK AI ko bhejna chahiye?

Kabhi nahi. OTP, password, UPI PIN, CVV ya full card details kisi ko bhi share na karein—AI bot ko bhi nahi.

Kya reported cyber-fraud amount ka matlab utna paisa confirm chori hua?

Reported amount, confirmed loss, frozen amount aur refunded amount alag metrics hain. Har figure ke saath uska source aur period dekhna zaroori hai.

SochSathi ki information kaise verify karun?

Har research topic par diye gaye original sources aur official portals ko khud check karein. Data ki reporting date aur limitations bhi padhein.

Online Rahiye, Hoshiyar Rahiye

Kisi bhi online offer, message, link ya earning claim par bharosa karne se pehle uski jaanch zaroor karein.

1. Claim Par Sawaal Karein

Guaranteed profit, double money aur instant income jaise claims ko bina jaanch accept na karein.

2. Evidence Check Karein

Screenshots aur testimonials ke alawa independent sources aur official information bhi dekhein.

3. Personal Data Bachayein

OTP, password, UPI PIN, CVV ya sensitive personal information kisi ke saath share na karein.

4. Digital Arrest Se Darein Nahi

Video call par police ya agency ban kar dhamki dene wale ko paise transfer na karein. Call kaat kar official channel se khud verification karein.

5. Fraud Ho Jaye To Turant Action

Financial cyber fraud mein turant 1930 par call karein aur cybercrime.gov.in par report karein. Bank ko bhi jaldi inform karein.
