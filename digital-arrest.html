Digital Arrest Scam

Fraudster police, CBI, ED, customs ya court officer ban kar call/video call par dhamki de sakta hai. Phone/video call par aisi 'digital arrest' ka kanooni adhikar nahi hota.

1. Ye kya hota hai?

Fraudster police, CBI, ED, customs ya court officer ban kar call/video call par dhamki de sakta hai. Phone/video call par aisi 'digital arrest' ka kanooni adhikar nahi hota. Har case alag ho sakta hai; neeche diya example samjhane ke liye kalpanik hai.

2. Step by step: aam taur par kaise hota hai?

  1. Parcel/Aadhaar/bank case ka bahana
  2. fake ID/uniform
  3. arrest ka dar
  4. call par rehne aur kisi ko na batane ka pressure
  5. verification/bail/security ke naam par payment demand.

3. Example se samjhein

Caller video par police officer dikhakar kehta hai ki kisi ko phone mat karo aur paise transfer karo. Call kaat kar official police channel se verify karein.

Ye kalpanik example hai, kisi specific company ya vyakti par aarop nahi.

4. Apna mind control kaise karein?

Uniform, official language aur family ko dhamki panic paida karte hain. Call kaatna aapka haq hai; panic mein paisa na bhejein.

  1. RUKO: Turant payment ya details na dein.
  2. SAANS: 10 dheemi saansein lein; phone kuch der side rakhein.
  3. VERIFY: Official website/number khud dhoondhkar check karein.
  4. DISCUSS: Trusted insaan ko batayein.
  5. DECIDE: Pressure khatam hone ke baad hi faisla karein.

5. Kaise bachein?

  • Video call par dhamki ko proof na maanein.
  • Paise, OTP, bank details ya screen sharing na dein.
  • Trusted person aur official police channel se independently verify karein.

6. Agar fraud ho gaya ho to?

  1. Paise transfer hue hon to turant bank/payment app ko inform karein aur transaction dispute/freeze request karein.
  2. India mein financial cyber fraud ke liye 1930 par jaldi call karein; phir cybercrime.gov.in par complaint karein.
  3. Chats, phone numbers, URLs, transaction IDs aur screenshots evidence ke roop mein sambhalein.
  4. Immediate physical danger ho to 112 par call karein.
