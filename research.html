RESEARCH SERIES • EPISODE 01

Colour Prediction Games: Paisa Kaise Banta Hai?

Colour prediction games mein player ek colour choose karta hai aur result ka intezaar karta hai. Lekin is system mein platform ki kamai kaise hoti hai? Isko samajhne ke liye payout, probability, rules aur incentives ko dekhna zaroori hai.

Is research mein hum jaanenge:

  • Game ka basic system kaise kaam karta hai?
  • Player ke payout aur platform ke revenue ka relation kya hai?
  • Probability aur expected loss ka matlab kya hai?
  • Bonus, referral aur VIP groups ka kya role ho sakta hai?
  • Kaun se warning signs par dhyan dena chahiye?

Research status: Pehla topic chuna gaya hai. Detailed research aur source verification abhi baaki hai.
