Binary Trading

Binary options mein fixed time ke baad price upar/neeche hone par all-or-nothing payout ho sakta hai. Ismein high risk hota hai.

1. Ye kya hota hai?

Binary options mein fixed time ke baad price upar/neeche hone par all-or-nothing payout ho sakta hai. Ismein high risk hota hai. Har case alag ho sakta hai; neeche diya example samjhane ke liye kalpanik hai.

2. Step by step: aam taur par kaise hota hai?

  1. Ad/signal se platform par aana
  2. demo wins
  3. deposit aur trade size badhane ka pressure
  4. short expiry mein repeated trades
  5. loss ke baad recovery trade ya VIP signal ka offer.

3. Example se samjhein

Do trades jeetne ke baad teesri mein loss aur turant double amount lagane ka mann revenge-trading pattern ho sakta hai.

Ye kalpanik example hai, kisi specific company ya vyakti par aarop nahi.

4. Apna mind control kaise karein?

Countdown aur recent wins overconfidence paida kar sakte hain. 'Abhi nahi to kabhi nahi' pressure ko pehchanein.

  1. RUKO: Turant payment ya details na dein.
  2. SAANS: 10 dheemi saansein lein; phone kuch der side rakhein.
  3. VERIFY: Official website/number khud dhoondhkar check karein.
  4. DISCUSS: Trusted insaan ko batayein.
  5. DECIDE: Pressure khatam hone ke baad hi faisla karein.

5. Kaise bachein?

  • Relevant regulator ki official guidance/status check karein.
  • Guaranteed return aur personal account deposit se bachein.
  • Demo profit ko real earning ka proof na samjhein.

6. Agar fraud ho gaya ho to?

  1. Paise transfer hue hon to turant bank/payment app ko inform karein aur transaction dispute/freeze request karein.
  2. India mein financial cyber fraud ke liye 1930 par jaldi call karein; phir cybercrime.gov.in par complaint karein.
  3. Chats, phone numbers, URLs, transaction IDs aur screenshots evidence ke roop mein sambhalein.
  4. Immediate physical danger ho to 112 par call karein.
