GAME KE PICHE KA GAME • AWARENESS GUIDE

Sextortion & Blackmail

Private image/video ya fake recording ke naam par paise ya aur content ki dhamki di ja sakti hai. Victim ki galti nahi—blackmail karna galat hai.

1. Ye kya hota hai?

Private image/video ya fake recording ke naam par paise ya aur content ki dhamki di ja sakti hai. Victim ki galti nahi—blackmail karna galat hai. Har case alag ho sakta hai; neeche diya example samjhane ke liye kalpanik hai.

2. Step by step: aam taur par kaise hota hai?

  1. Stranger friendship/flirt
  2. private call/chat
  3. recording ka daawa
  4. family ko bhejne ki dhamki
  5. money ya more content demand.

3. Example se samjhein

Unknown account paise na dene par family ko video bhejne ki dhamki de. Evidence save karein aur trusted person/authorities ki help lein.

Ye kalpanik example hai, kisi specific company ya vyakti par aarop nahi.

4. Apna mind control kaise karein?

Shame aur fear victim ko akela karte hain. Aapko blame karna galat hai; trusted person ko batana blackmailer ki power kam karta hai.

  1. RUKO: Turant payment ya details na dein.
  2. SAANS: 10 dheemi saansein lein; phone kuch der side rakhein.
  3. VERIFY: Official website/number khud dhoondhkar check karein.
  4. DISCUSS: Trusted insaan ko batayein.
  5. DECIDE: Pressure khatam hone ke baad hi faisla karein.

5. Kaise bachein?

  • Payment ya aur content bhejne se pehle help lein.
  • Chats, usernames, numbers aur demands ka evidence save karein.
  • Platform report karein; evidence save karne ke baad block karein.

6. Agar fraud ho gaya ho to?

  1. Paise transfer hue hon to turant bank/payment app ko inform karein aur transaction dispute/freeze request karein.
  2. India mein financial cyber fraud ke liye 1930 par jaldi call karein; phir cybercrime.gov.in par complaint karein.
  3. Chats, phone numbers, URLs, transaction IDs aur screenshots evidence ke roop mein sambhalein.
  4. Immediate physical danger ho to 112 par call karein.

7. TRUST CHECK AI

Suspicious link, message ya screenshot ka initial risk assessment lene ke liye bot use karein. Result 100% guarantee ya official investigation nahi hai.
