Lottery, Prize & Courier Fraud

Fake lottery/prize/parcel calls mein winnings ya parcel release ke liye tax, customs, insurance ya processing fee maangi ja sakti hai.

1. Ye kya hota hai?

Fake lottery/prize/parcel calls mein winnings ya parcel release ke liye tax, customs, insurance ya processing fee maangi ja sakti hai. Har case alag ho sakta hai; neeche diya example samjhane ke liye kalpanik hai.

2. Step by step: aam taur par kaise hota hai?

  1. Prize/parcel ka unexpected message
  2. fake receipt/tracking
  3. release fee
  4. ek fee ke baad doosri fee
  5. payment ke baad contact gayab.

3. Example se samjhein

Courier release ke liye ₹3,000 maange to courier ki official website par tracking khud verify karein.

Ye kalpanik example hai, kisi specific company ya vyakti par aarop nahi.

4. Apna mind control kaise karein?

Free prize ki khushi ya parcel ka dar jaldi decision karwata hai. Pehle verify karein.

  1. RUKO: Turant payment ya details na dein.
  2. SAANS: 10 dheemi saansein lein; phone kuch der side rakhein.
  3. VERIFY: Official website/number khud dhoondhkar check karein.
  4. DISCUSS: Trusted insaan ko batayein.
  5. DECIDE: Pressure khatam hone ke baad hi faisla karein.

5. Kaise bachein?

  • Enter na kiye prize ke liye advance fee na dein.
  • Courier tracking official website par khud check karein.
  • Unknown number/personal UPI par tax ya customs fee na bhejein.

6. Agar fraud ho gaya ho to?

  1. Paise transfer hue hon to turant bank/payment app ko inform karein aur transaction dispute/freeze request karein.
  2. India mein financial cyber fraud ke liye 1930 par jaldi call karein; phir cybercrime.gov.in par complaint karein.
  3. Chats, phone numbers, URLs, transaction IDs aur screenshots evidence ke roop mein sambhalein.
  4. Immediate physical danger ho to 112 par call karein.

7. TRUST CHECK AI

Suspicious link, message ya screenshot ka initial risk assessment lene ke liye bot use karein. Result 100% guarantee ya official investigation nahi hai.
