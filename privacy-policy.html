Privacy Policy

Last updated: 11 November 2026

SochSathi (“hum”, “hamari website”) online awareness aur educational information provide karta hai. Is policy mein bataya gaya hai ki website use karte waqt information ko kaise handle kiya ja sakta hai.

1. Information jo aap share karte hain

Website par diye gaye Telegram link ya kisi third-party service ka use karte waqt aap apni marzi se information share kar sakte hain. Filhaal website par koi account registration form ya direct payment form nahi hai.

2. Website usage

Website hosting provider GitHub Pages basic technical information, jaise request logs, apni service aur security ke liye process kar sakta hai. Is processing par GitHub ki policies bhi lagu hoti hain.

3. Third-party services

Hamari website Telegram aur GitHub jaise external platforms ke links deti hai. Jab aap un links par jaate hain, to un platforms ki apni privacy policies aur terms lagu hoti hain. SochSathi un third-party platforms ke data practices ko control nahi karta.

4. TRUST CHECK AI

TRUST CHECK AI ek alag Telegram-based service hai. Bot ko bheje gaye messages, links ya files ki processing bot aur uske service providers ke through ho sakti hai. Sensitive information, OTP, password, UPI PIN, CVV ya private documents share na karein.

5. Cookies

SochSathi website filhaal apni taraf se advertising ya analytics cookies set karne ka feature provide nahi karti. Hosting ya linked third-party services apni technologies use kar sakti hain.

6. Children

Website ka content general awareness ke liye hai. Bachchon ko online services use karte waqt parent ya guardian ki guidance leni chahiye.

7. Policy updates

Website ya services badalne par is policy ko update kiya ja sakta hai. Updated version isi page par publish hoga.

8. Contact

Privacy se jude sawaal ke liye SochSathi Telegram par message kar sakte hain.

Note: Yeh ek initial privacy notice hai. Kisi naye form, analytics, advertising, payment ya data-collection feature ko launch karne se pehle policy ko uske hisaab se update karein.
